SG LEDDim

Status

A status poll reads the dimmers' levels over Bluetooth. Without it, Homey only shows what the app itself sent. Polling also makes control slower — if the app becomes slow or unreliable, turn it off.

Keep the connection open

Experimental. Normally the app connects for each command, which takes a few seconds. With this on, it stays connected for a while after a command, so the next ones are fast. While it is connected, the SG phone app may not be able to reach the dimmers, and status polling waits.

Logging

Commands and status updates in the app log. Errors are always logged.

Full BLE and GATT tracing. Only turn this on while troubleshooting.
