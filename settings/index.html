SG account

Required if you have LEDDim Smart 3.0 or no first-generation LEDDim Smart devices, otherwise optional. Fetch the network password and device names. Your SG password is not stored.

SG account login is not included in this build.

Connection

Enable for faster response. If you have only one Gen1 device, the SG app on your phone cannot reach it while this setting is on.

Holds the link through the strongest device and asks one device every 4 seconds. For testing only: more Bluetooth traffic, and the SG app on your phone cannot reach that device while this is on.

Enable for faster response. If you have only one 3.0 device, the SG app on your phone cannot reach it while this setting is on.

Signal to Homey

Open a dimmer and check "Link". "Direct" means the dimmer has contact with Homey, and the others are reached through it. Weak signal? Move Homey closer to a dimmer.

Which SG devices are supported
  • LEDDim Smart: First generation (Gen1, @NDxxxx) has been tested and works. Later generations, including TW, Master and 400 2-pole, are being tested.
  • LEDDim Smart Pill, including Micro, TW and Smart Pill ON-OFF: being tested.
  • LEDDim Smart DIN, including DIN 1000 and TW: being tested.
  • Smart Plug, Smart Socket and Smart DIN 3600: being tested.
  • HeatReg Smart: the temperatures, the target temperature, on and off and the preset in Homey.
  • SG Smart 3.0: LEDDim Smart 3.0 has been tested and works. LEDDim Smart 2-pole 3.0 is being tested. Both need you to log in with your SG account. Other 3.0 products are not supported yet.
  • Other SG devices, such as Push, Floor and Pill DALI, are not supported yet.

Something not working? Report it in the forum thread.
