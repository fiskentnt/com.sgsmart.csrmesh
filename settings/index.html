Connection

Faster control, and changes made at the wall switch appear immediately. Only works with first-generation LEDDim Smart (@NDxxxx). If you only have one such dimmer, the SG Smart app on your phone cannot reach it while this setting is on.

Signal to Homey

Shown under Status when you open a first-generation dimmer. "Direct" means the dimmer is connected to Homey. Other dimmers are reached through it. Weak signal? Move Homey closer to a dimmer.

Which SG devices are supported
  • LEDDim Smart: First generation (@NDxxxx) is stable. Later generations, including TW, Master and 400 2-pole, are being tested.
  • LEDDim Smart Pill, including Micro, TW and Smart Pill ON-OFF: being tested.
  • SG Smart 3.0, the products with 3.0 in the name, is not supported yet.
  • Other SG devices, such as DIN, Push, Floor and Pill DALI, are not supported yet.

Something not working? Report it in the forum thread.

Troubleshooting

Only enable when the developer asks. Turn it off before sending a diagnostics report.

Shows the latest log lines. Bluetooth addresses and device names are hidden, but the log shows when lights are used. Send it as a private message to the developer in the forum, not in the thread.
